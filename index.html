Site settings


Page width

"Full width" is the fix for elements that looked full-width in the editor but left empty space on the sides of the real site — it lets your percentage-based layout fill whatever screen it's on. Pick a boxed width instead if you want a traditional centered column.

Top bar look

The top bar shows your site title (and logo, if set) with a link to every page that isn't hidden (toggle the eye on a page tab to hide one). Turn on "transparent until scroll" to have it float invisibly over your page and fade in its background once someone scrolls down.

Footer

Every page can end with this footer. The "Powered by Invisi" badge is free to remove for now — once monthly plans launch, keeping it off will require an active subscription.
